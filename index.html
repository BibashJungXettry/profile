Deal terms & proof policy Read before you deal

  • No refund policy. Once money is committed under agreed terms, it is not returned.
  • Mid-deal account sale. If a customer sells or loses the ID while a deal is still open (for example because the deal is taking longer than expected), another account must be provided. Money already paid is not refunded.
  • Extra charges. Long or complicated deals may require additional admin fees. Those are confirmed before work continues.
  • Official proofs only. The proofs we choose to provide (vouches, comments, and documents when applicable) are the official ones. Requests for live camera checks, opening webcams, or external videos that are not part of our terms will not be done.
  • Non-official verification requests from customers do not replace the proofs listed here and will not be treated as a requirement to prove legitimacy.
